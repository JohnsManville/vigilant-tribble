## 5. Clearance test — the hard gate nobody has cleared

Still nothing found on either Mac. Repeating it here because in Workstream A it is not a
paperwork gap, it is an **occupancy gate**:

**A mold remediation with no post-remediation clearance test should not be closed up behind
drywall, and Nancy should not move back in on the strength of it.** Sealing unverified
remediation inside a finished wall converts a testable problem into a destructive one.

Rose City Labs (503-307-0096) already has the pre-remediation baseline (E215). They are the
natural choice for clearance, and it is **expressly covered** — the policy's Additional Property
Coverage 10(d) covers testing *"whether performed prior to, during or after removal, repair,
restoration, or replacement."*

> **Get the clearance test before drywall.** It is covered, it is cheap relative to the job, it
> protects Nancy, and it closes the biggest hole in Workstream B at the same time. This is the
> clearest example in the whole project of one action serving both workstreams.
