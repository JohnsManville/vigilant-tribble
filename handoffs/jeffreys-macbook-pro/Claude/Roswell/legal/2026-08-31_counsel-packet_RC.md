## 6. My working read, for you to check or correct

1. **The deed change is not the problem.** An Oregon bargain and sale deed carries
   an implied covenant against encumbrances created by the grantor. A quitclaim
   carries none. Read as a mild improvement in my favor, not a red flag.
2. **The escrow cut is the substantive change.** It moves roughly $11,500 to
   $13,500 of mortgage payments onto me and pulls my full carrying date forward
   by six months.

## 7. The timeline this creates

Assuming a Sept 1, 2026 escrow start (unconfirmed, and this is the single most
load bearing unknown):

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Sept 1, 2026 - Feb 1, 2027 | Six escrowed mortgage payments run |
| Dec 31, 2026 | Nancy's utility obligation ends |
| Jan 1, 2027 | Utilities become mine; mortgage still escrowed |
| Feb 1, 2027 | Last escrowed mortgage payment |
| Mar 1, 2027 | First mortgage payment out of my pocket; Nancy's access ends; deed records |

**The pinch:** From Jan 1 to Mar 1, 2027 I pay utilities on a property Nancy
still has the right to enter to remove belongings. From Mar 1, 2027 I carry
everything.

**The larger constraint:** I need the renovation finished (target October 2026),
my family moved out of our current home on Malcolm and into Roswell, and Malcolm
sold or rented, before roughly March 2027. If that slips I am carrying two
mortgages against a business that ran negative in 2025.
