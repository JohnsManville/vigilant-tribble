### Opening

"I am a business and real estate attorney, and I represent Nancy Mumford. Nancy has asked me to write to you both because she has reached an important decision about the Roswell Street house, and she wants you to hear it clearly, completely, and in writing. I represent only Nancy in this matter; you are welcome, and encouraged, to have an attorney of your own review this letter."

### Nancy's decision

"As you know, Nancy has not lived in the house since June 2025, having lived in a care facility until December 2025, and has not been able to move back in as planned for January 2026. Her financial planner has advised her, and she agrees, that she cannot afford to move back into the house or to continue carrying its costs indefinitely. Nancy has therefore decided that it is time for her to step away from ownership of the house entirely. She wants to do this in a way that is generous to you both and that gives you every opportunity to succeed with the property."

### The five terms

**1. Transfer of Nancy's ownership interest to both of you.** Quitclaim deed conveying her entire interest to the two of them together. "Nancy specifically wants her interest to go to both Jeffrey and Laura, jointly." Signed deed held by the escrow company, recorded at the end of the twelve-month escrow period. Once recorded, they own the house one hundred percent.

**2. Twelve months of mortgage payments, fully funded.** Nancy deposits approximately **$24,000** with a title company or an independent Oregon escrow company, to be determined. "Beginning September 1, 2026, or as soon as practical, the escrow company will pay the monthly mortgage payment of approximately **$1,911** (which includes property taxes and insurance) directly to CMG Mortgage each month for twelve consecutive months. These funds will flow directly from the escrow company to the lender."

**3. Utilities covered through the end of 2026.** Water, sewer, garbage, and electric through December 31, 2026. "Effective January 1, 2027, Nancy's name will come off all utility and other property-related accounts, and those accounts, and their costs, will be yours to maintain."

**4. Nancy's personal property.** "Nancy still has valuable furniture and other belongings in the house, as well as additional belongings stored in the **10' x 20' Dry Box storage container** alongside the house. Nancy will have approximately six months, through **March 1, 2027**, to remove her personal property in an orderly manner, with access arranged at reasonable times. Until her belongings are removed, she asks that nothing of hers be moved off-site or disposed of."

**5. The end of Nancy's financial involvement.** "Nancy's financial involvement ends when she signs the quitclaim deed and funds the escrow account for the twelve mortgage payments described above. After that, Nancy will not make, and should not be expected to make, any additional payments, advances, loans, gifts, or other financial contributions relating to the property... **Because the existing loan is in Nancy's and Jeffrey's names, it will remain a legal obligation of both of them until it is refinanced or paid off**; however, as between Nancy and the two of you, responsibility for the property and its debt will belong entirely to you going forward."

### What this means for you

"Taken together, this offer gives you one hundred percent ownership of the house and a twelve-month runway with the mortgage fully covered: time to finish the renovation and then rent the house, refinance the loan, or sell the property, whichever path you choose. Nancy's intent is simple: to set you up for success, and to remove herself as a business partner in the property so that she can go back to simply being your mom."

### Next steps

"If these terms are acceptable, please let me know in writing (email is fine) by **August 24, 2026**. Once you confirm, I will prepare a short, plain-English agreement documenting these terms, together with the quitclaim deed and the escrow instructions. **If I do not hear from you by that date, Nancy intends to proceed with this plan as outlined, including signing the quitclaim deed, setting up an escrow arrangement, and shutting down utilities at the end of this year.**"

"Jeffrey, your uncle, **Patrick Todd**, holds Nancy's power of attorney for real estate matters and is copied on this letter. He is available to you if you would like to talk through the practical side of any of this. And of course, you are welcome to contact me with questions at any time."

### Closing

"This letter deals with a business matter, but it comes from a place of care. Nancy loves you both, and nothing in this letter changes that. Her hope is that a clean and generous resolution of the house allows everyone in the family to move forward."

## Flags

1. **The Aug 11 letter was misaddressed** to `jeff@odd-jobs.com` (.com, not .org). It did not reach Jeff until Lauren Lewis resent it on **Aug 13**. Any argument about the Aug 24 deadline should start there: actual notice was 2 days late out of a 13-day window.
2. **The Aug 24 deadline passed with no response on file.** The Aug 28 revision does not restate a deadline, and its closing line asserts they will proceed regardless.
3. **The Dry Box container is Nancy's**, 10' x 20', alongside the house, holding her belongings. Earlier sessions flagged the recurring $145/mo Dry Box invoices to jeff@odd-jobs.org as a possible claim or pack-out expense. Per this letter the container holds Nancy's personal property. Who actually pays that invoice is worth confirming before it is treated as either a claim item or a cost of the transfer.
4. **The CMG loan is in Nancy's and Jeffrey's names**, and Dale states plainly it stays a legal obligation of both until refinanced or paid off. That matches the refinance-intent position already in the counter proposal.
5. **Patrick Todd is Jeff's uncle** and holds Nancy's POA for real estate. He is offered as a back channel: "He is available to you if you would like to talk through the practical side of any of this."
6. **Laura is a named party** on both letters at `lauramumford1@gmail.com`. Any reply or agreement runs to her as well.
