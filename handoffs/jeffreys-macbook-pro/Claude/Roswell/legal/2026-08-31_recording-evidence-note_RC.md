## What exists

A voice memo recorded during a kitchen and bathroom walkthrough at 2870 SE
Roswell between Jeff and a collaborator, Matt. Recorded openly; Jeff states on
the recording that it is running and being transcribed.

## Why it matters

The counter proposal (Section 5.2) asserts that **Nancy elected finishes above
the insurance-funded scope and agreed to pay the difference.** I identified
that as the strongest claim in the compensation section because it is a
specific promise about a specific thing.

**The recording says something different.** Discussing the appliance package,
Jeff says in substance:

> Nancy came into money unexpectedly after a period of being poor, including
> insurance policies and bank accounts she had forgotten she held. His response
> to her was, in his own words, to buy the nice things.

And, describing the appliances: **"she bought the fanciest new appliances."**

## The problem, stated plainly

1. **It reframes who wanted the upgrade.** Section 5.2 says she chose above
   scope and owes the delta. The recording has Jeff encouraging the spend.
2. **"She bought" conflicts with the payment record.** Both Home Depot orders
   (cabinets H4017-565922, appliances H4017-567029) were charged to Jeff's Amex
   ending 1000 and shipped to Jeffrey Mumford. Either "she bought" means she
   chose, or it means her bridge advances funded them. Either reading weakens a
   claim that she still owes the delta.
3. **It compounds the bridge loan problem.** If her advances funded the
   appliances, the finish delta may already have been paid, in the form of the
   $59,771.38.

## What to do

- **Do not transmit Section 5.2 as written.** It asserts the opposite of what
  the recording says.
- **Tell counsel the recording exists** before he sends anything. Discovering it
  later, from the other side, is far worse than disclosing it now.
- **Establish whether other copies exist.** Matt has the same conversation in
  memory and may have his own recording.
- If the finish delta is pursued at all, it has to rest on documents rather than
  on the characterization of a conversation, and the documents currently point
  the other way.

## Also on the recording, non-legal but relevant

  and that it affected his capacity on this project. Useful for the lost-time
  reconstruction.
- **Nancy's health history** is discussed. Sensitive; keep out of any document
  that circulates.
- **A family remark** attributing Nancy's illness to the mini-splits Jeff
