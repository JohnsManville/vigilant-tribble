## Why it is written this way

- **Concedes nothing.** Explicit non agreement sentence up front. Every question
  is a request for a fact or a document, not a negotiating position.
- **Names your counsel without naming him.** Signals you are represented, which
  changes how opposing counsel writes to you, without inviting direct contact
  until you want it.
- **Gets the clock.** Questions 1 and 6 are the two that decide how much time
  you actually have. Everything downstream (reno finish, Malcolm decision,
  move) hangs on the escrow start date.
- **Forces the payment reconciliation.** Question 5 resolves the $1,911 versus
  $2,246.17 ambiguity using their records instead of yours.
- **Does not ask for the 12 months back.** That is a substantive position and
  belongs to your attorney after review, not to a holding letter.
