## 1. Scope of work

**1.1 Emergency response and mitigation.** Source identification, moisture mapping, containment, negative air, structural drying and monitoring.

**1.2 Mold remediation.** Removal of affected building materials, HEPA cleaning, antimicrobial treatment, and third-party post-remediation clearance testing. Occupant is immunocompromised; the standard applied was actual clearance, not a passing test.

**1.3 Contents.** Full pack-out, inventory, off-site storage in a 10' x 20' container, cleaning of salvageable contents including individual restoration of collectible items, and return.

**1.4 Reconstruction.** Kitchen and bathroom rebuild, drywall hang, tape and finish, cabinetry and appliance installation, finish carpentry, paint and punch.

**1.5 Mechanical and electrical.** Whole-house mini-split HVAC with per-room temperature sensors. Kitchen and bath rewire, panel evaluation (200A Gould ZQC-24 MVB, four open spaces), and circuit additions.

**1.6 Project administration.** Scheduling, procurement, subcontractor sourcing and coordination, permitting, and full insurance claim management including scope negotiation, adjuster meetings, reinspections and settlement.

## 5. Assumptions

- Work is limited to the scope described above.
- Hard costs marked "documented" come from invoices, carrier records and the project ledger. Items marked "estimated" are reconstructed from scope and market rate and are subject to substantiation.
- Materials are shown at cost. Odd Jobs' standard 30 percent markup is not applied.
- Labor hours derive from 86 documented working days at eight hours. Actual days ran longer.
- Weekend and after-hours work is billed at the standard rate, not the published 2x.
- The owner elected finishes above the scope and grade the insurance claim funded and agreed to pay the difference. That difference is inside the cabinetry and appliance line and is not separately marked up.
- Any concealed damage, additional repairs, or changes in scope would require written approval or a change order. Numerous selection changes occurred during this project without change orders and without schedule relief.

## 6. Exclusions

- Attic insulation and the vermiculite layer beneath it. Presumed asbestos, untested. **Nobody should enter that space until it is tested.** Not priced, not performed.
- Landscaping, fencing, and exterior work unrelated to the loss.
- Any work performed by the owner directly.
- Time spent on mediation or on the current legal negotiation. That is not construction work and is not billed.
