## WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS

This is the written accounting promised in the 13 July 2026 email "Purcell deck — acknowledgment of termination." It sets out what was planned, what was agreed, and what happened, with every statement tied to a primary record: the contract documents, supplier quotes and receipts, bank and payment records, text messages, emails, and photographs.

It is a record, not an argument. It computes no demand and draws no legal conclusion. Where the underlying records are incomplete, in conflict, or silent, the text says so in place rather than resolving the gap. Any error of record identified in this document will be corrected in writing against the underlying source.

**Conventions.**
- Every line carries its citation. Bracketed paths are relative to the ODHS project-records package; copies of the cited records are available on request. All times are Pacific.
- **CONTESTED** marks a point where primary records conflict. Both versions are reported; neither is resolved here.
- **UNKNOWN** marks a fact the primary records do not establish.
- **ESTIMATE** marks a figure not taken directly from a document; its basis is stated where it appears. Nothing else in this document is estimated.
- Abbreviations: **AGREEMENT** = "4 SOURCE DOCUMENTS/Purcell Lisa Darin - DECKadent Dreams 20x20 TREX DECK (2).pdf" (executed agreement, 5 pp). **DESIGN** = "2 EVIDENCE/03_Trex_Design_of_Record/TrexShoppingList-2.pdf"; **BLUEPRINT** = same folder, TrexBlueprint.pdf. **PROPOSAL** = "2 EVIDENCE/01_Contract_and_Proposals/Purcell DECKadent Dreams 20x20 copy.pdf" (18 Oct 2025, unexecuted). **HD R-/Q-#####** = receipts and quotes in "2 EVIDENCE/02_Home_Depot_Quotes_and_Order/". **EMAILS** = "2 EVIDENCE/05_Key_Emails_Exhibit/Key_Emails_Exhibit.md" (Gmail message ids given). **LEDGER** = the ODHS financial-records database (finrecon.db), transactions table, cited by row id.
